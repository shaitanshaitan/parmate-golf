Why we built it

Every scramble used to start the same way. Four of us on the first tee, one phone calculator going round, and the same questions every time. Whose 35 percent? Which tee? What was the slope again? By the time we agreed, the next flight was already waiting.

Scramble is the most sociable way to play golf. The maths should not get in the way. So Parmate does it for you, the way the World Handicap System says it should be done, and then keeps the score, so the only thing left to argue about is that putt on 7.

Parmate is made in Amsterdam by golfers who play it every week, and tested round after round on real Dutch courses.

Robbie, founder of Parmate

From first tee to final putt

Set up a round while you wait to tee off. Four screens, and you are playing.

  1. Choose your game

    Team Round, Team Match, 1vs1 or Solo. Then pick your course and the loop you are playing.

  2. Add your team

    Pick buddies or add a guest with their handicap index. The WHS scramble allowance is set for you, or choose your own.

  3. Get the team handicap

    Course handicap for every player at their own tee, the allowance applied, one team number. Done.

  4. Score hole by hole

    Par and stroke index on every hole, your score against par as you go. Save the round and share the card.

Check the maths yourself

This is the calculation Parmate runs for every team. Change a handicap index or the tee and watch the team handicap move.

Team handicap

Course handicap = index × slope ÷ 113 + (course rating − par). Players are sorted from lowest to highest course handicap, each gets their allowance, and the total is rounded once at the end.

Team handicap5

Worked example: on a tee with course rating 70.2, slope 122 and par 71, players with handicap index 8.2 and 14.5 have course handicaps of 8.05 and 14.85. With the WHS scramble allowance of 35% and 15% that is 2.82 + 2.23 = 5.05, so the team handicap is 5.

Allowances follow the WHS recommendation for scramble (Rules of Handicapping, Appendix C): 35/15 for two players, 30/20/10 for three, 25/20/15/10 for four. In the app, rating, slope and par come straight from the course list for every tee, for men and women. Read the full guide.

Get Parmate on your phone

Parmate runs straight from your browser and installs to your home screen like any other app. No app store, no download to wait for.

Android

  1. Open play.parmate.golf in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots) at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app, or Add to Home screen.

iPhone

  1. Open play.parmate.golf in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.

Questions golfers ask

Is Parmate free?

Yes. Setting up rounds, the team handicap, scoring and saving your rounds are free. A few extras, like the hole map, are part of Parmate Pro, which you can upgrade to inside the app.

How does Parmate work out a scramble team handicap?

It calculates each player’s course handicap from their handicap index and the rating, slope and par of the tee they play. Then it sorts the players from low to high and applies the scramble allowance, 35/15 for a team of two by default as the WHS recommends. You can also set your own percentages. Read the full guide.

How are handicap strokes given in a 1vs1 match?

In a net match each player gets 100% of their course handicap. The player with the lowest handicap plays off zero and the other gets the difference, one stroke on each hole from stroke index 1 upwards. How match play strokes work.

Which courses are in Parmate?

Golf courses in the Netherlands for now, with more on the way. See the course list. Missing yours? Ask for your course or add it yourself in the app.

Do I need an account?

You sign in with Google or with your email address. That way your rounds, teams and buddies are saved and you can play live matches with friends.

Does it work on iPhone?

Parmate runs in the browser on any phone. It is built and tested on Android. iPhone should work, but has not been fully tested yet. Let us know if you run into anything.

Which languages does Parmate speak?

The app and this website are in English and Dutch. Bekijk de Nederlandse website.
