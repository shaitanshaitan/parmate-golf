Website privacy notice

What happens with the details you send us on parmate.golf. The Parmate app has its own privacy policy.

Updated 28 November 2026

Who we are

Parmate is made by The Ninth Tee, Amsterdam, the Netherlands (KvK 98566903). Questions about your data: legal@theninthtee.eu.

What we collect on this website

  • Course request form: the course name, town and country, the loops or tees you play and, if you fill it in, your email address. We use this to add the course to Parmate and, if you left your email, to tell you when it is in the app.
  • Newsletter: your email address, your language and your consent. We use this only to send you the Parmate newsletter.

The legal basis is your consent (newsletter) and our legitimate interest in answering your request (course form).

Who processes it

The forms are handled by Netlify, Inc., which hosts this website and stores the form entries for us as our processor. Course requests are also emailed to our own mailbox. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Course requests are kept until the course is handled, and at most 12 months.

Unsubscribe and your rights

Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link, or email us and we remove you. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, at legal@theninthtee.eu. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Cookies

This website sets no tracking or advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and photos from Unsplash.

The app

For your account, rounds, payments and everything else in the app, see the Parmate app privacy policy.
